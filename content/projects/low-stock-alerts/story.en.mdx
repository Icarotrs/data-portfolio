## The scenario

A typical small online store: stock lives in a spreadsheet updated by hand, nobody tracks how fast each product sells, and suppliers need 7 to 21 days to deliver. A product running out is noticed when customers complain.

## How I'd build it

- An import of the last 30 days of sales from the store's CSV export into a **Stock** tab.
- Per product: average daily sales, **days of cover** (how long current stock lasts), a **reorder point** from the supplier's lead time plus a one-week buffer, and a **suggested order** that covers 45 days.
- A status for every SKU (*OK*, *Reorder soon*, *Reorder now*) shown with an icon and a label, not colour alone.
- An Apps Script trigger will run every morning at 7am and **email purchasing** the products to reorder.
- A **Reorder log** tab will record every alert, and a **Settings** tab will hold lead times, thresholds and recipients.

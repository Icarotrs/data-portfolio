## Problem

An online store had more than 5,000 reviews across Amazon and Shopify. The team read some of them, but nobody could say which problems came up most, whether they were getting better, or what to fix first.

## What I built

- A Python script that sends each review to an **LLM** with a fixed prompt and gets back a **topic**, a **sentiment** and a **one-line summary** in a strict format.
- Results written to **Google Sheets** with a **confidence score**; anything under 80% is flagged for a quick human check.
- A **hand check** of 300 random reviews to measure accuracy (94% agreement) before trusting the numbers.
- A dashboard with **sentiment by topic**, the top complaints with real quotes, and the trend after each change (the new packaging cut negative reviews by 4 points).
- New reviews are tagged on a schedule, so the dashboard stays current.

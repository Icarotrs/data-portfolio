## The scenario

A typical online store: revenue goes up every month, but nobody knows which products actually make money. Shipping, marketplace commission and payment fees sit in different exports, so "best sellers" and "most profitable" get treated as the same list. They aren't.

## How I'd build it

- A clean data model joining orders, items, products, payments, freight and sellers: 8 tables from the Olist dataset, a public dataset from a Brazilian marketplace (values in BRL).
- A **real margin** measure per order line: price − freight − marketplace fee − payment fee − product cost.
- A product cost table with a simulated cost per category, clearly marked as **simulated**, so it can be swapped for real costs with one file.
- Three report pages: monthly P&L, product ranking by net margin, and an order-level drill-down.
- Conditional formatting that flags every product with negative margin.
- A fourth page, **Marketing**, joining simulated ad spend from Google, Meta, TikTok and email to the orders: spend, attributed sales, **ROAS** and cost per new customer per channel. With simulated ad spend, the Marketing page will flag which channels return under 3× ROAS.

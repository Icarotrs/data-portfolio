## The scenario

A typical online store with thousands of reviews from Amazon and Shopify in a Google Sheet, and more arriving every week. Reading them all by hand takes days, so nobody does, and the team can't say which problems come up most or what to fix first.

## How I'd build it

- An **Apps Script** workflow inside the same Google Sheet: a custom menu (*AI tagging → Tag new reviews*) plus a nightly trigger for new rows.
- Reviews will be sent to the **Claude API** in batches of 25 with a fixed prompt. Each one will come back as strict JSON (**topic**, **sentiment**, a **one-line summary** and a **confidence** score) and be validated before it is written.
- Results will be written **straight back into the sheet**, next to each review. Rows under 80% confidence will be flagged for a person to check.
- Apps Script limits how long one run can take, so large sheets will be processed in parts: progress is saved and a trigger resumes the work until every row is done.
- A **hand-check** tab with 300 random reviews, to measure agreement with a person before trusting the results.
- The tagged sheet will feed a small dashboard of sentiment by topic.

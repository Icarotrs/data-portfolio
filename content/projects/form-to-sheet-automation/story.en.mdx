## The scenario

A common setup: order requests come in through a Google Form, and someone copies rows by hand into the real sheet every day, risking formulas and formatting. The weekly summary is put together by hand every Friday.

## How I'd build it

- An Apps Script `onFormSubmit` trigger will read each response, clean it (trimmed text, normalized phone and date formats) and append it to the **formatted** table, keeping formulas, validation and conditional formatting intact.
- A lock (`LockService`) will make sure two simultaneous submissions never write to the same row.
- A summary tab will be driven by formulas (totals per week, per product, per status).
- A time-driven trigger will export the summary tab as a **PDF** every Monday at 8am and email it to the owner.
- A **Settings** tab will let the owner change recipients and schedule without touching code.

## Problem

Every supplier sent their price and stock list in a different shape: one as a CSV with semicolons and European decimals, another as a PDF table, another with headers merged across two rows. Dates came as `03/04/24`, `2024-04-03` and `April 3`. Someone spent hours each week retyping and fixing these files before they could be compared.

## What I built

- A parser for CSV, XLSX and text-based PDF tables that detects delimiters, encodings and multi-row headers.
- A normalization step: one date format, one currency format, trimmed and title-cased text, split/merged columns fixed, duplicates removed.
- A column-mapping file, so a new supplier format is a few lines of configuration instead of new code.
- Output to Google Sheets: a clean data tab, a **summary tab** (counts, totals, price changes vs. last import) and a **log tab** listing every fix that was applied.
- The same rules available as an Apps Script menu inside Google Sheets for non-technical users.

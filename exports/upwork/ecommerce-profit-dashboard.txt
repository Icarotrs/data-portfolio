TITLE
E-commerce Profit Dashboard

DESCRIPTION (466/600 characters)
Power BI profit dashboard for an e-commerce store, built on the public Olist dataset (~99k orders). It calculates real net margin per product and per order after shipping and marketplace fees, and flags SKUs that sell at a loss. Includes a monthly P&L view, product ranking and drill-down to order level. Product cost is simulated and clearly labeled. Stack: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, SQL, Python. I can build the same for your Shopify, Amazon or WooCommerce data.

SKILLS
Power BI, DAX, Power Query, SQL, Python (pandas)

RESULT
Real net margin for ~99k orders and 32k products, with loss-making SKUs flagged in one view.

TITLE
Shopify → Sheets → AI → Alerts

DESCRIPTION (511/600 characters)
n8n automation for a Shopify store: every new order hits a webhook, gets logged to Google Sheets and is classified by AI (Claude API) for fraud risk, shipping priority and customer type, with a one-line reason. Risky, rush and VIP orders trigger a Slack message and an email so the team acts before shipping. Saves about 5 hours a week of manual order checks (estimate). Demo uses simulated test orders; the importable n8n workflow JSON is included. Stack: n8n, Shopify, Google Sheets, Claude API, Slack, Gmail.

SKILLS
n8n, Shopify webhooks, Google Sheets, Claude API, Slack, Gmail

RESULT
Each order is logged and classified within about a minute; risky, rush and VIP orders reach the team before they ship.

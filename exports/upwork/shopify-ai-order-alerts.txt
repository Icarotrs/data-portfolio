TITLE
Shopify → Sheets → AI → Alerts

DESCRIPTION (528/600 characters)
n8n automation for a Shopify store: every new order hits a webhook, is logged to Google Sheets and classified by AI (Claude API) for fraud risk, shipping priority and customer type, with a one-line reason. Orders flagged as high risk, rush or VIP go to the team on Slack and email for review before fulfillment, as a second check next to Shopify's own fraud analysis. Designed to save about 5 hours a week (estimate based on 40 orders a day at about 1 minute each). Importable n8n workflow, test orders and setup guide included.

SKILLS
n8n, Shopify webhooks, Google Sheets, Claude API, Slack, Gmail

RESULT
Orders flagged as high risk, rush or VIP are sent to the team for review before fulfillment. A second check, not a replacement for Shopify's own fraud analysis.

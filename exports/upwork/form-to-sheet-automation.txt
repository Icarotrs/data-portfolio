TITLE
Form-to-Sheet Automation

DESCRIPTION (527/600 characters)
Demo project, build in progress. Google Forms to Google Sheets automation with Apps Script: each response will be cleaned and written into a formatted sheet without breaking formulas, dropdowns or formatting, with a lock against simultaneous submissions. A weekly summary will be exported as a PDF and emailed every Monday, with a Settings tab for recipients. Designed to save about 3 hours a week (estimate based on 20 responses a day at about 2 minutes each). Fictitious data. Stack: Google Forms, Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail.

SKILLS
Google Forms, Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail

RESULT
Designed so every response lands in the right row with formulas intact, and a PDF summary goes out every Monday.

TITLE
Form-to-Sheet Automation

DESCRIPTION (444/600 characters)
Google Forms to Google Sheets automation with Apps Script: every response is cleaned and written into a formatted sheet without breaking formulas, dropdowns or formatting, the thing the default form link always breaks. A weekly summary is exported as a PDF and emailed every Monday. No add-ons or paid tools. Saves about 3 hours a week of copy-paste (estimate). Demo uses fictitious data. Stack: Google Forms, Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail.

SKILLS
Google Forms, Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail

RESULT
Zero manual copy-paste: every response lands in the right row and a PDF summary goes out every Monday.

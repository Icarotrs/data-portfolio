TITLE
Form-to-Sheet Automation

DESCRIPTION (444/600 characters)
Google Apps Script automation that writes Google Form responses into a formatted Google Sheet without breaking existing formulas, validation or formatting (the default form link overwrites them). Each response is cleaned, written to the right table row, and a weekly summary is generated as a PDF and emailed automatically on a time trigger. No add-ons, no paid tools. Demo uses fictitious data. Stack: Apps Script, Google Sheets, Forms, Gmail.

SKILLS
Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Google Forms, Gmail / PDF export

RESULT
Zero manual copy-paste: every response lands in the right row and a PDF summary goes out every Monday.

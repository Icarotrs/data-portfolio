TITLE
Inventory Reorder Alerts

DESCRIPTION (468/600 characters)
Inventory reorder system in Google Sheets + Apps Script for a small online store. It imports daily sales, calculates days of stock left, reorder points from supplier lead times and a suggested order quantity per product, and colour-codes every SKU (OK / reorder soon / reorder now). Every morning a script emails purchasing the products that will run out before a new order can arrive. No paid apps. Demo uses fictitious data. Stack: Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail.

SKILLS
Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail, Shopify CSV export

RESULT
Stock-outs caught 1–3 weeks early, with a suggested order quantity for every product.

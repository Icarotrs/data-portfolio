TITLE
Low-Stock Alerts

DESCRIPTION (456/600 characters)
Low-stock alerts in Google Sheets + Apps Script for a small online store. Daily sales are imported, and every product gets days of stock left, a reorder point based on supplier lead time and a suggested order quantity. Every morning a script emails purchasing the products that will run out before a new order can arrive, 1 to 3 weeks ahead (simulated data). No paid apps; works with a Shopify or any store export. Stack: Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail.

SKILLS
Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail, Shopify export

RESULT
Stock-outs are caught 1 to 3 weeks early, with a suggested order quantity for every product.

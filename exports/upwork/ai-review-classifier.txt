TITLE
AI Review Classifier

DESCRIPTION (520/600 characters)
AI integration inside Google Sheets: an Apps Script workflow sends new customer reviews to the Claude API in batches and writes the topic, sentiment and a one-line summary straight back into the sheet. It runs from a custom menu or on a schedule, and rows with low confidence are flagged for a person to check (94% agreement with a hand check). 5,214 reviews tagged in about 3 minutes instead of roughly 2 days of reading (estimate). Demo uses fictitious reviews. Stack: Google Sheets, Apps Script, Claude API, Power BI.

SKILLS
Google Sheets, Apps Script, Claude API, Power BI

RESULT
Runs from a menu in Google Sheets or on a schedule; 94% agreement with a 300-review hand check, and low-confidence rows go to a person.

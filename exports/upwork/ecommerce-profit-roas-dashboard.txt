TITLE
E-commerce Profit & ROAS Dashboard

DESCRIPTION (539/600 characters)
Demo project, build in progress. Power BI dashboard that shows real profit, not just revenue: net margin per product and per order after shipping, marketplace and payment fees, with loss-making products flagged. A Marketing page will compare simulated ad spend and sales per channel (ROAS, cost per new customer) and flag channels under 3× ROAS. Built on the public Olist dataset from a Brazilian marketplace (~99k orders, values in BRL); product cost and ad spend are simulated and labeled. Stack: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, SQL, Python.

SKILLS
Power BI, DAX, Power Query, SQL, Python (pandas)

RESULT
Real net margin per order and per product, with loss-making products flagged. With simulated ad spend, the Marketing page will flag which channels return under 3× ROAS.
